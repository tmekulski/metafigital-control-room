METAFIGITAL · PEOPLE'S FACTORY

Community Demo Script

A standalone demo artifact for Loudoun County supervisors, REIT boards, and community groups. Each phase stands alone — you don't need the full stack to demo phase one.

If it doesn't self-improve, it doesn't move forward. If it doesn't have a receipt, it didn't happen.

Phase 1 · Weeks 1–4 · The Gate

Stand up the Sandbox on one Constitution

No products, no agents — just the gate. Put a live terminal in front of an Ashburn community group and show what no clause, no action looks like when someone tries to bypass it.

Phase 2 · The Money Shot

Prevention before the accident, in public

Run one product through Concept and the Founder's Forge. Seed it with bad data, a power-loss event, an override attempt. Watch it reject on screen and slide back to the Sandbox. That's the differentiator: governance before failure, not forensics after.

Phase 3 · The Fail on Camera

One agent through training, turned back

Shared Sim, Individual Training, Workforce Training. Fail the practicum on camera. Turn it back. The Ombudsman records it from the rail — human hold authority, community hold ready.

Phase 4 · The Seal

Survivors meet at the Configital OS Toolbox

Turing collects tickets, issues MetaCons, seals the bus. Human team boards in the Game Room. Kill switch confirmed live.

Phase 5 · The Human Stamp

TRU-VU Authorization

Four checks: intent, authority, observed behavior, safety. A real person stamps it. Not a checkbox — a signature.

Phase 6 · The Build

MetaBuilder takes the handoff

Apprentice's shovel breaks ground on a physical pad. Retail operations module runs. The second physical training module closes the GRSI loop with accreditation — governed recursive self-improvement proven in the field, not simulated.

Phase 7 · The Receipt

Scout's bag shows thirteen steps

TRU-Print complete. Community reads the receipt. Every MetaCon is independently checkable — what was done, under what authority, against which clause.
